## Immediate actions (worker who witnesses a collision or uncontrolled vehicle)

1. Move to a safe distance, accounting for the fact that a rail wagon or heavy vehicle may still be moving even if it appears to be slowing.
2. Do not approach a vehicle involved in a collision until it is confirmed stationary and, if applicable, its engine/power is off.
3. Alert others in the vicinity, particularly anyone in the vehicle's path of travel.
4. Report immediately: type of vehicle(s) involved, whether anyone appears trapped or injured, whether the vehicle is carrying any hazardous material, and whether the road/track is blocked.

## For the response team

1. Confirm the vehicle is fully stopped and, where relevant, secured against rolling (chocks, brakes engaged) before anyone approaches.
2. If the vehicle is carrying hazardous material and there are signs of a breach (leak, spill, damaged container), treat it as a combined traffic-and-chemical/gas incident — follow the relevant material-specific procedure in addition to this one.
3. If someone is trapped, do not attempt extraction without appropriate equipment and trained personnel; stabilizing the person and the vehicle takes priority over speed.
4. Redirect other traffic away from the site immediately — a secondary collision at an unmarked incident site is a real and common risk.

## After the incident

- The site should not be cleared/cleaned until initial investigation (photos, positions, witness statements) is complete, unless clearing is necessary to prevent further danger.
- Full incident report including vehicles involved, cause if apparent (mechanical failure, human error, signaling failure), and whether hazardous cargo was involved.

## Prevention principles worth noting in any investigation

- Pedestrian and vehicle routes should be physically separated where possible; where they cross, crossing points should be clearly marked and have adequate visibility.
- Reversing vehicles require a spotter in areas with pedestrian traffic.
- Speed limits on internal roads/yards should be enforced, not just posted.
