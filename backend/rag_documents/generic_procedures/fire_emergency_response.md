## Immediate actions (worker who discovers a fire)

1. Raise the alarm immediately — activate the nearest fire alarm/call point, then verbally alert people nearby.
2. Only attempt to fight the fire yourself if **all** of the following are true: it is small and contained (e.g., confined to a waste bin or small equipment fire), you have been trained on the specific extinguisher type required for that fire class, and you have a clear, unobstructed escape route behind you.
3. If any of the above is not true, **do not attempt to fight the fire** — evacuate and let trained responders handle it.
4. Never fight a fire involving gas cylinders, electrical equipment (unless using a rated extinguisher), or unknown chemicals.

## Evacuation

1. Move calmly to the nearest safe exit, using stairs, not elevators.
2. Close doors behind you where possible to slow fire spread, without locking them.
3. Proceed to the designated assembly point (see Evacuation & Assembly Point Procedure) and remain there until a headcount confirms you're accounted for.
4. Do not re-enter the building/area for any reason until the fire authority declares it safe.

## Extinguisher class reference

| Class | Fuel type | Suitable extinguisher |
|---|---|---|
| A | Ordinary combustibles (wood, paper, cloth) | Water, foam, dry powder |
| B | Flammable liquids | Foam, CO₂, dry powder |
| C | Flammable gases | Dry powder (isolate gas source first) |
| D | Combustible metals | Specialist dry powder only |
| Electrical | Live electrical equipment | CO₂ or dry powder only — never water |

## For designated fire wardens / response team

1. Confirm the alarm is genuine, identify the fire's location and apparent scale.
2. Ensure evacuation is underway before any fire-fighting attempt.
3. Direct trained fire-fighting personnel; do not allow untrained personnel to attempt suppression of anything beyond a small, contained fire.
4. Account for all personnel at the assembly point; report any unaccounted person to the incident commander immediately — do not send anyone back in informally.
5. Liaise with external fire services on arrival; hand over incident details (what's burning, hazardous materials involved, known casualties).

## After the fire is controlled

- Do not permit re-entry until a competent authority confirms structural safety and no residual hazard (smoldering material, damaged electrical systems, weakened gas lines).
- File a full incident report, including cause if known, response timeline, and any injuries.
